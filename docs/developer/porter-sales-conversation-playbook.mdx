# Porter sales conversation playbook

This playbook defines the public, source-backed conversation patterns that can
be used in Porter's commercial voice agent without importing third-party call
recordings or unsupported claims.

## Sources evaluated

- Gong's public analysis of 300M+ cold calls reports that a tailored,
  permission-based opener should lead with context, acknowledge the cold call,
  and ask permission to continue:
  https://www.gong.io/blog/the-best-and-worst-cold-call-openers-backed-by-data-from-300m-calls
- HubSpot's 2025 survey of 379 sales professionals found that most script users
  adapt their scripts, and identified research, personalization, active
  listening, adaptability, and strategic questioning as important skills:
  https://blog.hubspot.com/sales/state-of-cold-calling
- Salesforce's public campaign playbook recommends three to five focused
  discovery questions, explicit objection handling, and clear handoff criteria:
  https://www.salesforce.com/en-us/wp-content/uploads/sites/4/documents/partners/gtm-offerings/isv-agentforce-marketing-playbook.pdf
- Gong's Call Playbook code is MIT licensed, but its gated conversation dataset
  is licensed only for non-commercial academic research. Its license forbids
  incorporating the dataset into a product or service:
  https://huggingface.co/datasets/gong-io-research/call-playbook

## Porter implementation rules

1. Use one permission gate. After consent, move directly into a short value
   statement and one discovery question. Do not ask for permission twice.
2. Treat scripts as guarded talk tracks. Locked facts, disclosures, rates, and
   compliance language remain deterministic; acknowledgements and transitions
   may vary within approved boundaries.
3. Ask one question per turn. Reflect a meaningful prospect answer before the
   next question, without generic praise or filler.
4. Answer factual interruptions from the locked Porter knowledge, then resume
   the pending discovery question.
5. Handle an objection once, listen to the answer, and either continue,
   schedule a callback, hand off, or close respectfully. Never loop pressure.
6. End with a concrete next action and collect only the contact and
   qualification fields required for that action.
7. Use only consented Porter conversations for product evaluation or future
   training. Redact PII, retain source and consent metadata, and keep test and
   production corpora separate.

## Current call shape

`context and disclosure -> permission -> value plus discovery -> current
financing -> pain -> volume -> advisor handoff -> confirmed close`

Global interruptions such as rates, identity, AI disclosure, callbacks,
voicemail, wrong person, and stop requests take priority over the current
discovery stage.
